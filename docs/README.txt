# WanderVista Group Tours website

Open `index.html` in a modern browser.

Included:
- Responsive premium travel landing page
- 15 destination cards
- Click-to-expand country galleries and attractions
- Upcoming Japan + South Korea section
- Two 20-second YouTube video previews
- Contact phone and email
- Mobile-friendly layout

Notes:
- The website uses remote image/video assets so the page stays lightweight.
- Before commercial publication, verify image/video licensing and replace any assets with your own licensed photography/videos or assets from official tourism media libraries.
- Replace the placeholder brand name "WanderVista" with your preferred business name/logo.
